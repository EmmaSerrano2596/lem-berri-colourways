Product 02 · New & improved · Tapping

Berri

"Berri doesn't rush. It builds."

Berri is a raspberry-shaped clitoral massager that taps instead of buzzing or sucking. The tapping starts soft and builds slowly, so it's made for longer sessions and "edging" (getting close, easing off, and building again). It's a great pick for people who find Lem too intense.

It comes with 3 swappable tapping caps. Short cap = stronger tap, medium = balanced, long = softer.

How it works

  1. 1
    Charge it. 90 minutes (USB-C) gives up to 2 hours of play.
  2. 2
    Pick a cap. Start with the medium one.
  3. 3
    Turn it on. Hold the button for 2 seconds. It taps gently to say hi.
  4. 4
    Build, pause, return. Short presses move through 12 settings. Start low and work up.

Quick answers

FAQ

Are these new products?

No. Lem and Berri are existing products. Only the colours are new. Specs, settings, materials and how they feel are exactly the same.

Which one should a first-timer get?

Lem is our go-to beginner pick: one button, easy to use, and it works fast. Berri suits people who want a slower build or who find suction too strong.

Can they be used in the shower or bath?

Yes. Both are IPX7, which means fully waterproof. Rinse with warm water and mild soap after use.

Are they insertable?

No. Both Lem and Berri are for external use only.

What lube works with them?

Water-based lube only. Silicone lube can damage the silicone surface.

Are they loud?

No. Both run under 50 dB, which is quieter than a normal conversation.

Is shipping discreet?

Yes. Orders ship in plain packaging, and the card statement shows "SP HELLO NANCY" or "SP RCH NANCY".

Can customers buy a specific new colour on its own?

To confirm with the launch team. At launch, the new colours first appear in the Superfruit Bowl blind box, where the Lem colour is a surprise.
